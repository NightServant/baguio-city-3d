- Measure:
  - 128,327 buildings kept, 148 dropped inside landmark rings.

- **Design change:**
  - **Detail moved into the app's shader.** Vertex RGB = roof colour; alpha = wall colour index × 64 + the floor's height mod a storey. The shader draws windows per 3 m bay × 3.2 m storey, rows level from the floor, and ribs on pitched roofs, fading below pixel size.
  - Walls and roofs share their corners, and no texture or UVs ship.
  - Tiles split by quadtree: near from z15 at ≤ 700 buildings; far from z13 at ≤ 4,000.
- **Far LOD:**
  - under 60 m² dropped (sub-pixel at z13.5);
  - 60 to 200 m² roof only;
  - larger buildings as boxes on the minimum rectangle;
  - 12-bit positions.
  - The app loads far tiles only within 1.7 km × 2^(14 − zoom), which is 2.4 km at the default view.
- **Result:**
  - near: 724 tiles, p50 11.1 KB, p95 44.9 KB, max 58.4 KB (11.2 MB in total, 1.80 M triangles);
